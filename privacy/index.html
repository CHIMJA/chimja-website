Privacy Policy

How CHIMJA handles information across its websites and products.

Effective date2 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CHIMJA handles information when you visit our website or use a CHIMJA product that links to this policy. Individual products may publish additional product-specific privacy information where necessary.

Information we collect

We may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, support request, account information, or other details submitted through a CHIMJA service. Our websites and products may also collect limited technical information needed to operate, secure, improve, and understand the service.

How we use information

We use information to provide and improve our products, respond to requests, maintain security, prevent abuse, comply with applicable legal obligations, and communicate about a service when appropriate.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may use service providers that help us operate CHIMJA products, subject to appropriate obligations, and we may disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the relevant purpose, subject to legal and operational requirements. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about privacy, access, correction, deletion, or other rights that may apply to you under applicable law.

Children

Unless a product explicitly states otherwise, CHIMJA services are not intended for children under the age at which parental consent is required by applicable law.

Changes

We may update this policy as CHIMJA and its products evolve. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
