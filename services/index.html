06 / BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful answers.

What kinds of projects can we discuss?

Native iOS applications, company websites, web applications, and startup MVPs. Share the problem, intended users, and current stage so we can assess whether the project is a good fit.

Can we collaborate internationally?

Yes. CHIMJA is based in Bengaluru, India and is available for remote collaboration with clients worldwide. Communication channels and workable meeting times are discussed around your location and project needs.

How are project estimates prepared?

Estimates depend on scope, complexity, integrations, and the starting point. We first clarify the requirements and assumptions, then discuss an estimate and delivery approach. There is no universal price or timeline.

Who owns the finished work?

Ownership, source-code handover, and any third-party licensing are defined in the project agreement before work begins. Share any specific ownership requirements during the initial discussion.

Is maintenance available after launch?

Maintenance, updates, and additional development can be discussed separately. The scope, availability, and commercial terms of ongoing support are agreed for each project.

How do I get started?

Email hello@chimja.com with a short overview of your idea, the service you need, and any budget or timing constraints. You can also use the project inquiry form below once its submission configuration is complete.
